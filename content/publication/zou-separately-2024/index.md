abstract: Current end-to-end coreference resolution models combine detection of singleton
  mentions and antecedent linking into a single step. In contrast, singleton detection
  was often treated as a separate step in the pre-neural era. In this work, we show
  that separately parameterizing these two sub-tasks also benefits end-to-end neural
  coreference systems. Specifically, we add a singleton detector to the coarse-to-fine
  (C2F) coreference model, and design an anaphoricity-aware span embedding and singleton
  detection loss. Our method significantly improves model performance on OntoNotes
  and four additional datasets.
